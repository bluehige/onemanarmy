## Shared visual contract

- Warm bone paper, hard black/white value structure, crisp pen line on eyes, hands, chain, swords, locks, cup, and evidence.
- Broad broken dry-brush strokes for cloth, rain, timber, cliffs, smoke, and motion.
- Two or three controlled gray washes; no all-over speckle, glossy digital finish, RPG HUD, tactical grid, or magic aura.
- Dried-blood red is optional and stays below five percent.
- Negative space and eye direction carry emotion before sword quantity does.
