# Screen contracts

## Title

- Player question: continue this record or open a new one?
- Primary action: choose one route into the story.
- P0: title and Continue/New Game. P1: Load. P2: settings/credits/quit.
- Art remains the dominant field; navigation is one restrained vertical register, not a card dashboard.

## Story

- Player question: what does Lee Yeon hear, believe, or say next?
- Primary action: advance dialogue or choose a response.
- P0: speaker, dialogue, progress affordance. P1: active choices. P2: log/auto/skip/save/settings.
- Scene art occupies at least 70%; the dialogue surface targets 24-26% after the V2 redesign.

## Focus interlude

- Player question: what does Lee Yeon notice first?
- Primary action: select one of two to four real scene targets.
- P0: scene, focus outlines, one-line instruction. P1: short observation. P2: optional additional targets.
- One target is sufficient; no hidden-object failure or central modal.

## Narrative choice / intent

- Player question: which consequence takes priority?
- Primary action: choose one explicit cost, then optionally hold or pull to embody the decision.
- P0: two to four choices with direct gain and cost. P1: irreversible warning. P2: history.
- Input skill never changes the authored result.

## Formation cinematic

- Player question: how is the chosen principle being executed?
- Primary action: watch, pause, summarize, or skip to an understandable consequence.
- P0: art and choreography. P1: a short opening purpose. P2: controls on demand.
- Persistent UI stays under 10%; no combat values or success banner.

## Consequence

- Player question: what was gained, lost, and left behind?
- Primary action: inspect or continue.
- P0: aftermath image and at most four result lines. P1: blade recall. P2: archive detail.
- No score, XP, rank, morality meter, or battle report composition.
